# Speech and data (research module)

**Owner:** M6.

Maintain Arabic ASR/TTS and Egyptian dialect evaluation plans, a synthetic scenario/response/safety benchmark, dataset/license register, and specialist content-review coordination. Use synthetic or properly authorized data; verify release-specific license, consent and redistribution terms before use. Speech transcripts must be visible and correctable, with text/AAC alternatives always available. Raw child audio/video is not stored by default.

The planning specification proposes 100–200 structured scenarios and 300–500 synthetic example dialogues as targets, not existing assets or required final counts. Keep source/reviewer, age band, skill, acceptable response patterns and use restrictions for each item. Include ambiguity/clarification and specialist-reviewed safety cases; separate development, validation and test splits, and keep test data out of prompt tuning. Report dialect/noise conditions and task-specific transcription accuracy or WER. Make an evidence-based month-five go/no-go recommendation; speech cannot delay the core MVP.
